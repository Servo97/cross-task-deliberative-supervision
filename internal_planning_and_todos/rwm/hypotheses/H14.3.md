# H14.3 — Mixing domains improves the encoder

**Claim (as pre-registered):** adding a second domain's edges (rmb) improves RoboCasa retrieval
(E1b − ctrl-1Db > 0). **Weakest form the evidence supports:** none — the like-for-like contrast is
mixed-sign.

**Origin:** plan §2/§6 (cross-domain corpus as the point of the method); A3 made the bridge a
measured decision.

**Status:** REFUSED as not worth resolving (2026-08-30). Recorded defect, not papered over.

**Evidence (status §20.5, ledger):**

| contrast | Δ | signs | reading |
|---|---:|---|---|
| E1b − ctrl-1Db, whole gate (pre-registered) | +0.0129 | 3/3 | **artifact — withdrawn**: ctrl-1Db changes the gate population (188 anchors @0.0084 vs 319 @0.0062), so the statistic measures that E1b can retrieve rmb↔rmb at all |
| E1b(rc→rc) − ctrl-1Db, like-for-like stratum | **−0.0116** | (+,−,−) | adding rmb does not improve RoboCasa retrieval; n ≈ 39 seeds/arm to resolve the observed effect |

Lesson banked: pre-register the stratum, not the whole-gate statistic, whenever an arm changes the
gate population.

**Would move it:** 39 seeds per arm — refused on cost; the multi-domain corpus is justified by
coverage (4 domains → one encoder serving all benchmarks), not by this contrast.
