# H14 — Markovianization via cross-task deliberative workspace supervision (root)

**Claim (weakest form the evidence forces today):** training the workspace encoder on cross-task
pairings mined by VLM deliberation over segment descriptors produces ω that retrieves functionally
matching moments across tasks on held-out episodes far above chance, and the typed pairing
structure is what produces it (offline metric; π0.5 frozen tap; 12k steps × batch 64; two domains).
**No memory-content and no policy claim is entailed yet.**

**Origin (user, 2026-08-22, adopted verbatim as the paper claim shape):** "a memory-demanding task
is one where the current observation is an insufficient state. The workspace token stream is
trained — via cross-task deliberative supervision — to be the missing sufficient statistic, so that
(current observation + GDN-state over long-context workspace tokens) ≈ a Markovian state for the
policy. The GDN read is the recurrent causal carrier of that statistic at post-train AND inference.
The LCR counterfactual becomes the test of Markovianization." Plan §0.0.
