# H14.1 — The deliberation STRUCTURE produces the retrieval effect (C2, offline)

**Claim:** with the same objective, budget and seeds, removing or scrambling the typed edge
structure destroys cross-task retrieval: the deliberative term is the whole effect. Scope: offline
top-1 retrieval on the A1d disagreement subset of cross-task pairs, held-out episodes, π0.5 frozen
pooled tap, RoboCasa (+ rmb in the multi-domain cells).

**Origin:** plan §6 pre-registered reading (ii) "E1 > max(ctrl-S, ctrl-T); if ctrl-T ≈ E1 the recipe
is multi-domain SupCon, not a deliberation result". Panel amendment A4 (honest attribution funnel).
