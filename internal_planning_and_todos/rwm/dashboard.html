Hypothesis tree

H14Markovianization via cross-task deliberative supervisionOPEN · policy level

Weakest claim the evidence forces: deliberatively mined cross-task pairings make ω retrieve functionally matching moments across tasks far above chance, and the typed structure is what produces it. Offline metric only; no memory-content or policy claim entailed.

Encoder stage sealed 2026-08-31. Every policy arm below is the arbiter of the stronger claim.

H14.1Structure, not a contrastive term, produces retrieval (C2)CONFIRMED · offline

E1b 16.25× chance vs ctrl-0b 0.97× (chance), ctrl-S 1.13×, ctrl-T 0.20×; E1b − ctrl-0b +0.089, 3/3 seeds. Seed spread ≈5 lift units is the error statement.

H14.2Deliberation-mined positives beat embedding positivesCONFIRMED · offline

+0.062 on the pre-registered statistic, 3/3 seeds, single-domain and confound-free (11.83× vs 4.03×); corroborated +0.049 in the two-domain corpus.

H14.3Mixing domains improves the encoderREFUSED

Like-for-like Δ −0.012 with mixed signs; the pre-registered whole-gate statistic was an artifact (gate population changed). ≈39 seeds per arm to resolve — not worth it.

H14.4ω carries memory content a history read can useCLOSED-NEGATIVE

Bound slots and hidden sides are perception (decodable from the raw frame tap). Progress state: 0/8 families beat a time-only clock under pooled-linear and GRU-64 probes. H_absent survives; next cell E3 (event-marked ω) designed, not run.
